How Collections Work

One link. Always in sync. Never out of date.

Collections bundle related assets into a single shareable workspace that updates itself as your library changes.

  • Auto-sync every asset that matches your rules
  • One share link with custom permissions
  • Role-based access for contributors and viewers
  • Live activity feed of every change
