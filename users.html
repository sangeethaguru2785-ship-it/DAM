Role Permissions

Granular access control

Full Control

Admin

Manage users, roles, collections, settings, and every asset in the workspace.

  • Upload & delete any asset
  • Manage team & billing
  • Full audit log access

Create & Edit

Editor

Upload, tag, version, and organize assets. Share collections with anyone.

  • Upload & edit assets
  • Create collections
  • Download approved originals

Read Only

Viewer

Browse, preview, and download approved sizes. No editing or sharing rights.

  • Search & preview
  • Download sized versions
  • Comment on assets

Limited Access

Guest

Time-boxed access to specific collections only, for external partners.

  • Access shared collections
  • Preview & download
  • No upload permissions

Choose the right access level

Start everyone at the least access they need, then scale up as trust grows. Admins manage the workspace, Editors create and publish, Viewers browse, and Guests get time-boxed access to specific collections.
