Who We Are

We’re designers and engineers who got tired of hunting for files.

Stackly started when two creative teams hit the same wall: gigabytes of brilliant work scattered across drives, chats, and inboxes — impossible to find the moment it mattered. So we built the home we wished existed.

Today Stackly unifies every asset a brand creates in one secure, searchable, infinitely shareable place — so creative teams stop managing disorganization and start shipping great work.

  • One source of truth for every image, video & document
  • Role-based permissions that scale with your team
  • Enterprise-grade security, consumer-grade ease

The Stackly journey

From two frustrated design teams to a platform trusted by 30,000+ teams worldwide.

2018

Two teams, one frustration

Two creative teams in LA and London watch campaigns stall while files get lost across drives, chats, and inboxes.

2019

Stackly v1 ships

Our private beta proves the core idea — a single, searchable home for every asset — with 2M+ assets under management within a year.

2021

The AI search breakthrough

We launch AI-powered visual and semantic search, cutting average find-time from hours to under 12 seconds.

2023

SOC 2 Type II & global CDN

Enterprise certification completes and a global CDN brings asset delivery to 99.9% uptime across 48 countries.

2026

30,000+ teams call Stackly home

Design, marketing, and product teams around the world manage 2M+ assets in one beautiful command center.
